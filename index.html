2. Quick size guide

LengthHow far the toy is designed to insert. Compare “insertable length,” not only total length.
DiameterWidth across the widest part. A few millimetres can change how roomy it feels.
GirthDistance around the shaft—different from diameter. Product pages should label it clearly.

Example: a 3 cm diameter means 3 cm across; the circumference around a circle of that diameter is about 9.4 cm. Diameter and circumference are not the same number.

Penis size: the simple, factual version

Size changes between soft and erect, and natural variation is wide. A systematic review of professionally measured adults reported average erect length around 13.12 cm (5.17 in) and average erect circumference around 11.66 cm (4.59 in). Those are population averages, not a target and not a way to judge a partner. Read the research.

Measuring tip: length is measured from base to tip along the top side when erect; circumference is the distance around the shaft, not its width across. A product’s insertable length and diameter are not interchangeable.
