Penis size: the simple, factual version

Size changes between soft and erect, and natural variation is wide. A systematic review of professionally measured adults reported average erect length around 13.12 cm (5.17 in) and average erect circumference around 11.66 cm (4.59 in). Those are population averages, not a target and not a way to judge a partner. Read the research.

If an adult chooses to measure: when fully erect, place a straight ruler along the top side from the pubic bone/base to the tip (press only gently; do not cause pain). For circumference, wrap a flexible tape around the thickest part of the shaft. Circumference is the distance around, not the width across. Soft size can vary substantially and does not reliably predict erect size or sexual performance.

The review had 692 professionally measured erect-length observations and 381 erect-circumference observations, so treat the averages as context—not precision for every person.

Research does not establish a single standard size band for every dildo or butt plug. Toy size labels below are preference filters only; always read the manufacturer's exact dimensions and instructions.

Body-size basics · no shame, no guessing

“How do I check my vagina or butt size?”

Vulva / vagina

The vulva is the outside; the vagina is the flexible internal canal. Appearance and length vary naturally, and the canal changes with arousal. There is no reliable home “virginity test”: hymen appearance cannot prove sexual history. Do not insert a ruler or household object to measure yourself.

If insertion is persistently painful, or there is new itching, unusual discharge, bleeding or a strong change in smell, consult a qualified clinician.

Butt / anus

There is no everyday body-size measurement needed for the anus. Do not try to measure or stretch it with household items. For a toy, use the manufacturer's insertable length and maximum diameter as product dimensions—not a target for your body.

For anal toys, pick a small, easy-to-control design with a flared base, use plenty of suitable lubricant, clean as directed, and stop if uncomfortable.

Body shape, tightness and sexual history cannot be reliably inferred from how someone looks. Comfort, consent and health matter more than a number.

Sources: Cleveland Clinic: Vagina anatomy · Planned Parenthood: Sex toys & flared-base safety · Safer lubricant use.
